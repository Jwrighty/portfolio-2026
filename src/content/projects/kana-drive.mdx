Kana Drive is a mobile learning game for practicing Japanese kana through short challenge loops, visual feedback, and a driving theme shaped around my son's love of jeeps.

The project started as a personal study tool and became a way to explore how agentic workflows handle iterative product direction: changing the theme midstream, rebuilding interaction patterns, and keeping the learning mechanics legible for both adults and children.

It is still in active development, with the strongest through-line being the balance between language-learning utility and a playful interface that can hold a young learner's attention.
