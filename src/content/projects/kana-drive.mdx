A fun side project I am doing with my 4 year old son that leans on his love of all things 4x4 and attempts to make learning Hiragana and Katakana fun and engaging.

The project started as a personal study tool and became a way to explore how agentic workflows handle iterative product direction: changing the theme midstream, rebuilding interaction patterns, and keeping the learning mechanics legible for both adults and children.
It's also been fun to explore animations, assets, sprites etc.

It is still in active development as I go through play testing with my son. Adding features like cosmetic unlocks (think Jeep colors, roof racks, spoilers etc).
