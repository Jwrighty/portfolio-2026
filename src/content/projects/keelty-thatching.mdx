Keelty Master Thatcher is a site I built from the ground up for a traditional thatching business. The focus was a warmer, more tactile visual identity that keeps the craft itself centre stage, paired with a real emphasis on SEO and performance — fast loads, clean semantics, and strong Lighthouse scores across the board.

The site leads with service storytelling — re-thatching, re-ridging, and maintenance — backed by a gallery of completed work across the West Country, client trust signals, and direct contact paths. The visual system stays restrained so the photography of the trade does the heavy lifting.

Built with Astro, styled with Tailwind CSS and Radix UI primitives, with Framer Motion for considered, lightweight motion.
