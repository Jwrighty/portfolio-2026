Billingual grew out of a very specific friction point: paper bills in Japan can be stressful when the deadline, issuer, amount, and payment path are all locked inside unfamiliar language and formatting.

The app lets a user photograph a bill, extracts structured payment information with Claude Vision, explains how to pay it, and tracks upcoming deadlines with reminder emails. I built it as a full product surface for testing agentic workflows across planning, backend schema design, AI prompt constraints, UI polish, and end-to-end verification.

The current deployment is live, with planned follow-up work to remove bundled API-key assumptions, let users bring their own provider key, and open source the app as a fully free project.
